Guarantee check: **CRC mean test SWER 0.0450 <= alpha 0.05** (CRC bounds the expected risk;

## Findings
1. **The calibration guarantees hold.** CRC keeps expected severity-weighted error at 0.0450
   against a budget of 0.05. LTT stays under budget in 98% of splits against a promised 90%.
2. **Without humans, the budget is unreachable.** Even perfect routing between the two models
   reaches only 0.0654 (oracle), and no split was feasible without the flag action. Severity-3
   errors concentrate on MedMCQA, where the 14B model is right only 61% of the time. Meeting
   alpha = 0.05 needs about 28% human review (CRC), which is most of the cost.
3. **Against every SmartModelRouter baseline, the risk-aware router cuts severity-3 errors by half or more.** HSER falls from 0.178–0.274 to 0.089, and overall
   accuracy rises from 0.75–0.86 to 0.92. The price is a higher cost per query, mostly human review.
4. **Stakes-awareness adds no measurable gain at matched cost.** HSER 0.089 (ours) vs 0.090
   (stakes-blind at the same budget) vs 0.089 (stakes-blind ablation). The gain comes from
   calibration, verification and the human fallback, not from predicting severity. Likely
   reasons: 74% of queries are level 2; the stakes estimator reaches only 60% exact accuracy;
   and label agreement is fair (kappa 0.21), so severity is a noisy signal. Report this as a
   negative result, not as a win.
5. **Verification pays.** At the same risk, the verify action lowers cost by 12.6% (2.306 vs 2.637).
   Verifier precision is 1.00 for code and FAQ checks and 0.87 for numeric checks.
6. **Calibrating on the wrong labels breaks the guarantee.** Calibrating on LLM pre-labels or
   keyword rules, then evaluating on the workbook labels, gives SWER 0.0506 and 0.0554, above
   the 0.05 budget. The guarantee is only as good as the labels used to calibrate.
7. **The weights set the trade-off.** Steep weights cut cost by 35% (1.49 vs 2.31) but raise HSER
   to 0.124; flat weights lower HSER to 0.077 at 18% more cost.
8. **The SmartModelRouter confidence cascade fails with Qwen2.5-3B,** which reports confidence
   10 on 84% of queries, so the cascade sends almost everything to the large model.
9. **Distribution shift breaks the guarantee:** SWER is 0.105 on unseen Kanglish queries
   (n = 10; indicative only).

## Limitations
- Severity labels: author-supplied workbook with fair agreement (kappa 0.21, 300 overlap);
  raw per-rater records were not supplied. See `data/human/PROVENANCE.md`.
- No reference answers for most open questions (large answer used as reference); judge
  accuracy 88% on a 76-grade audit.
- The logistics (38) and Kanglish (10) slices are small; no new FAQ entries or Kanglish rows were added.
- Costs are list-price proxies; human cost is an assumption (20x large), not a measurement.
